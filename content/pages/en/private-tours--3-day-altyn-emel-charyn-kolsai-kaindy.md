3 days tour

> A private 3-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal driver-guide. We never combine travelers into group tours.

{{фото}}

### DAY 1

Almaty, Kaindy lake, Kolsai lake

8:00 meeting with the guide at the hotel

Departure

13:00 Arrival at Kolsai Lake

Exploring to the Lake (upper view points)

Lunch at Saty village

15:00 Departure to Kaindy Lake

16:00 Arrival at lake

Hiking/horseride

18:00 Accommodation in comfortable yurts in Saty village

19:00 Dinner

### DAY 2

Charyn Canyon, Singing dune

7:30 breakfast

8:00 departure to Charyn Canyon

Hiking in the Canyon 2 km or using local 4x4 taxi (optional)

14:00 Lunch in Chundja village (local cuisine)

16:30 arrival at the Basshi village

17:00 arrival on Singing dune (sunset view)

19:00 dinner in Aigai Kum restaurant

Accommodation in Aigai Kum hotel

### DAY 3

Ak Tau mountains, 700 years old willow, Almaty

7:30 breakfast

10:00 arrival at Ak Tau mountains

Excursion/Free time

Visiting 700 years old willow

13:30 Lunch at Basshi village

14:30 departure to Almaty

18:00 Arrival at Almaty
