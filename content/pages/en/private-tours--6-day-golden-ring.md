Departure

12:00 arrival at the Basshi village

Lunch

14:00 departure to the Ak Tau mountains

15:00 arrival at Ak Tau mountains

Excursion, hiking up to the dune

Visiting 700 years old willow

Arrival back to hotel

Accommodation

19:00 dinner

Altyn Emel, Zharkent, Hot springs

8:00 breakfast

10:00 arrival at Singing dune

Excursion to the top

Departure to Zharkent City

13:30 Lunch at Zharkent City

Visiting museum (Chinese mosque)

17:00 Arrival at hot springs

Accommodation

19:00 Dinner

Free time

Bestamak Canyon, Temirlik Canyon, Kaindy lake

8:00 breakfast

11:00 Exploring Canyons

Lunch

14:00 Departure to Kaindy Lake

16:00 Arrival at lake

Free time

18:00 Accommodation in Saty village (comfortable yurt or double room)

19:00 Dinner

Hike between Kolsai-1 and Kolsai-2: approximately 16 km return.

Kolsai lake1, Kolsai lake 2

8:00 breakfast

9:00 departure to Kolsai lake 1

14:00 Lunch

18:00 arrival back to Saty village

Dinner

Charyn Canyon, Assy plateau

8:00 Breakfast

9:00 Departure to Charyn Canyon

Exploring Canyon

Picnic at the canyon river

13:00 Departure to Almaty through high mountain Assy plateau

17:00 Arrive to Turgen gorge

Stay overnight

8:00 Breakfast

10:00 visiting waterfall

13:00 visiting Issyk Lake

14:00 Lunch at trout farm

17:00 Arruval back to Almaty city
