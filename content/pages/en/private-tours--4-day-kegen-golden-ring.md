4 days tour

> A private 4-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal driver-guide. We never combine travelers into group tours.

### DAY 1

12:00 meeting at Kegen’ boarder

14:00 Lunch at Saty village

15:00 visiting Kolsai lake 1

17:00 arrival back to Saty village

Accommodation in yurts

Dinner

### Day 2

8:00 breakfast

9:00 departure to Kaindy lake

Hiking to the lake 2 km or horse riding tour to the lake (optional)

Driving to Charyn Canyon

Exploring Canyon

14:00 Lunch in Charyn Canyon

15:00 departure to Hot springs in Cundja

18:00 arrival at Hot springs in Cundja

Accommodation

Free time

### Day 3

8:00 Breakfast

Last minute swimming

10:00 Driving towards Zharkent city

Exploring old Mosque museum in Zharkent

13:30 Lunch at Zharkent city

Driving towards singing dune

17:00 Sunset at singing dune

Dinner at hotel

### Day 4

8:00 breakfast

9:00 driving to Ak Tau mountains

10:30 Exploring Ak Tau mountains

13:30 arrival back to hotel

Lunch

15:00 driving back to Almaty

19:00 arrival at Almaty
