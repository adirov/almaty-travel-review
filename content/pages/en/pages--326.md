- Route 1: 1 hour, 13 km. Paved road: Alma-Arasan dam and Ayusai visitor centre.
- Route 2: 2 hours, 22–25 km. Paved road: Alma-Arasan springs, dam, Ayusai and waterfall.
- Route 3: 1.5 hours, 15–20 km. Off-road: Kazachka River gorge and panoramic meadow.
- Big Almaty Lake: 3 hours, off-road. Original passport or identity document required.

Drivers must be 18 or older on routes 1–3. Instruction, guide support and basic protective equipment are included.
