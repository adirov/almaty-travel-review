4 days tour

> A private 4-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal driver-guide. We never combine travelers into group tours.

### DAY 1

Altyn Emel

9:00 meeting with the guide at the hotel

Departure

12:00 arrival at the Basshi village

Lunch

14:00 departure to the Singing

Dune

15:30 arrival at the Dune

Excursion, hiking up to the dune

Arrival back to hotel

Accommodation

19:00 dinner

### DAY 2

Altyn Emel

8:30 breakfast

11:00 arrival at Ak Tau mountains

Excursion to the top

13:30 Lunch at Ak tau mountains

16:00 arrival at the 700 year old willow

17:00 Arrival at hotel Altyn Emel

19:00 Dinner

### DAY 3

Charyn Canyon, Kolsai lake

8:00 breakfast

12:00 Arrival at Charyn Canyon

Exploring to the canyon

Lunch

14:00 Departure to Kolsai Lake

16:00 Arrival at lake

Free time

18:00 Accommodation in Saty village (comfortable yurt or double room)

19:00 Dinner

### DAY 4

Kaindy lake, Almaty city

8:00 breakfast

9:00 departure to Kaindy lake

Hiking to the lake 2 km or horse riding tour to the lake (optional)

14:00 Lunch in Saty village

15:00 departure to Almaty

20:00 arrival at Almaty
