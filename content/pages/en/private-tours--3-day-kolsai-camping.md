3 days hiking tour with night spend in mountains

> The Kolsai Lakes National Park is the most popular destination in the Almaty region: a three-level cascade of Kolsai lakes and the separate Lake Kaindy. A private 3-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal driver-guide. We never combine travelers into group tours.

{{фото}}

### Day 1 (10 km hiking)

8:00 meeting in hotel/apartments

Departure to Charyn Canyon national park

11:30 arrival at Charyn Canyon national park

5 km hiking in the Canyon

13:30 departure to Saty village and Kaindy Lake

14:30 lunch at Saty village in guest house

16:00 arrival at Kaindy Lake

Hiking 5 km or horseback riding (additional payment)

19:00 arrival at Saty village

Accommodation in cozy yurts/dinner

### Day 2 (9 km hiking)

8:00 breakfast

8:30 departure to Kolsai lake 2

9:00 arrival at parking

9 km hiking, horse covering tents and equipment.

12:30 arrival at Kolsai lake 2

Lunch at the Lake (lunch boxes)

Setting up camp

Hiking to Kolsai lake 3

Resting chilling having a good time

Dinner

Accommodation in tents

### Day 3 (18 km hike )

8:00 breakfast

Departure to parking

12:00 Arrival at parking

14:00 Lunch by the way

18:00 arrival at Almaty
