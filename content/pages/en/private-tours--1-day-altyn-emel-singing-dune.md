1 day tour

> This off-road trip takes you deep into Altyn-Emel National Park to see and climb the legendary Singing Dune. Perfect for unique landscapes, desert photography and wildlife viewing. A private 1-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal driver-guide. We never combine travelers into group tours.

7:00 departure from Almaty

11:00 arrival at Basshi village

11:30 departure to Singing dune

Hike up/free time

14:00 lunch/picnic

16:00 Departure back to Almaty

20:30 Arrival back to Almaty
