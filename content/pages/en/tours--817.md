2 days tour

> A private 2-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal driver-guide. We never combine travelers into group tours.

### Day 1

8:00 meeting in hotel/apartments

Departure to Charyn Canyon national park

11:30 arrival at Charyn Canyon national park

2\.5 km hiking down the Canyon

13:00 lunch on the shore of the river in the Canyon,

going back on taxi

14:30 departure to Saty village and Kolsai lake 1

16:00 arrival at the Kolsai Lake 1

Free time/hiking/boating on lake/horseback riding

19:00 arrival at Saty village

Accommodation/dinner

### Day 2

8:00 breakfast

9:00 departure to Kaindy lake

10:00 arrival at parking

3km hiking or horseback riding to the lake

12:00 departure to Saty village

13:00 lunch at Saty village

14:00 departure to Almaty city, stop on the way at the view points of Black Canyon

18:00 arrival at Almaty city
