2 days tour

9:00 departure from hotel

13:00 arrival at Basshi village

14:00 lunch/accommodation

16:00 departure to Singing dune for sunset

19:00 arrival back to hotel

Dinner

Free time

8:00 breakfast

8:30 departure to Ak Tau mountains

9:30 arrival at Ak Tau mountains

Optional: hiking up to 2 hours

Free time

12:00 departure to 700 years old willow

Exploring willow/driving back to hotel

13:00 Lunch

14:00 departure to Almaty city

19:00 arrival back to Almaty
