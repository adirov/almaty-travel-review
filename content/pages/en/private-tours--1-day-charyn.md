1 day tour

> Explore one of Kazakhstan's most dramatic natural landscapes on a private day trip from Almaty, with your own vehicle and local driver-guide. A private 1-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal driver-guide. We never combine travelers into group tours.

9:00 meeting in hotel/apartments

Departure to Charyn Canyon national park

11:00 visiting Uygur market by the way

13:00 arrival at Charyn Canyon

Free time/hiking inside Canyon

Lunch/picnic near by the river (additional)

19:00 arrival at Almaty
