Private day trip from Almaty: hotel pickup, Big Almaty Gorge, lake viewpoints, a meal stop and return to your accommodation. A Sunkar falconry visit can be added. Pickup time and upper mountain transport are confirmed for your date.

## Trip outline

1. Hotel pickup in Almaty.
2. Journey through the Big Almaty Gorge.
3. Lake viewpoints and time for photographs.
4. A meal stop according to the agreed itinerary.
5. Optional Sunkar falconry visit.
6. Return to your hotel or apartment.
