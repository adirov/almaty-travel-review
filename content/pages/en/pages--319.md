We operate in Medeo gorge. The duration of the tour is 2h, 3h, 4h, 5–6h.

- Kim-asar gorge — 12 km. Butakovsky waterfall.
- Kok-zhailau — with climbing the spurs with a view of the city.
- Furmanov Peak — visit the famous Swing.
- Lake Manshuk Mametova — in the Maloalmatsky gorge, at 3550 metres above sea level.
- Upper Gorelnik — visit a hot spring.
- Chimbulak.
- 2-hour walk through pine and birch groves.

You will be accompanied by a guide responsible for the horses and your safety.
