### Day 1

8:00 meeting in hotel

Departure to Kaindy lake

12:00 arrival at parking

2 km walking path to the lake

14:00 departure to Saty village

Lunch at Saty village

15:00 departure Kolsai lake 1

16:00 arrival at the Kolsai Lake

Free time/walkibg around/boating on lake

19:00 arrival at Saty village

Accommodation/dinner

### Day 2

8:00 breakfast

9:00 visiting Black Canyon

10:00 Moon Canyon

11:00 Bestamak Canyon

12:00 Temirlik Canyon

14:00 Lunch in Charyn Canyon, red Castles valley, Tary restaurant

Accommodation in private 2 cabins Pana hotels, with facilities inside

Fresh up

17:30 walking up on sun set in the Canyon to the river

Drive back

Dinner

### DAY 3

9:00 Breakfast

Departure to Almaty

Visiting Bartogay Lake by the way

14:00 Arrival back to Almaty
