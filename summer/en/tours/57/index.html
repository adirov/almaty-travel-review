Charyn Canyon: Valley of Castles and ash grove

Visit the Valley of Castles in Charyn National Park. The park also protects a relict ash grove covering approximately 5,000 hectares. Canyon sightseeing and a visit to the grove are separate stops; the itinerary specifies which is included.
