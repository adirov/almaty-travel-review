Shymbulak Ski Resort

Added: 1 june 2018

 

 Shymbulak Ski Resort

At an altitude of 2,400 meters above sea level, nestled in the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains, lies one of the most picturesque places in Kazakhstan and Central Asia.

This is a wonderful destination that opens its doors to all winter sports enthusiasts, as well as those looking to relax and recharge in the heart of nature. An excursion to Shymbulak will reveal the scenic expanses of this area and allow you to visit the world-famous high-mountain Medeu Sports Complex. A wide variety of activities and highly professional instructors will help you make the most of your vacation, bringing you thrilling new experiences and unforgettable emotions. Whether you love ice skating, live for speed, or are eager to conquer new slopes on skis or snowboards — come to Shymbulak Ski Resort and make your dreams a reality. In the summer, you can discover this place from a whole new perspective and explore its beautiful landscapes on a mountain bike. If you want to take in the views from a bird's-eye view, try paragliding over the high-altitude Medeu ice rink. We will help you experience true relaxation, making you want to return again and again.

 

For beginners, we recommend working with our professional instructors, who will help you overcome fear and get comfortable on skis or a snowboard on your very first day.

Don't put your dreams on hold — give yourself and your family an unforgettable mountain getaway surrounded by nature.

Only with us can you experience a true rush of adrenaline, go skiing and snowboarding, and take exciting trips by mountain bike or paraglider.
