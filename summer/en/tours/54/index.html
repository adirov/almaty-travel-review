Lake Kolsay

Added: 12 january 2019

Tour Lake Kolsay — is a trip to the system of lakes, the lowest of which is located at 1818 meters above sea level, with a length of more than 1 km. This is the easiest and fastest way to Lake Issyk-Kul, which is located in Kyrgyzstan. The system of Kolsay lakes is located 300 km to the east of Almaty in the Kungey Alatau mountains.

The complex consists of several levels. The Second Kolsay Lake is the most beautiful, reaching two and a half kilometers in size. It is located 5 km away from the First Kolsai Lake. Special attention goes to the Upper Kolsay Lake, which is 4 km further and 600 meters higher than the others. It is virtually surrounded on all sides by rocks.

Program: After a long journey, you arrive at Lake Kolsay and get checked into the guest houses of your choice. Further in the plan, you will have dinner of your choice (you can discuss the menu in advance, as well as all questions about nutrition and picnics with our staff). The next day will be free until dinner. During this time, you can take a walk around the lake or go for a boat ride. We also organize horseback riding to the second lake and hiking trips to Sary Bulak.
