Kaindy Lake

Added: 18 january 2018

     Kaindy Lake — a truly amazing example of nature's creation. It is located among a vast pine forest at an altitude of more than 2,000 meters above sea level. It was formed by a huge limestone landslide that created a natural dam. The lake is quite large, reaching depths of over 30 meters. Once a forest stood in this spot, but now the ancient tree trunks emerge from the middle of the lake, giving it an extraordinary and surreal panorama.

    "Kaindy" in Kazakh means "abundant with birches." The unparalleled views of the lake harmoniously combine with the birch trees growing throughout the gorge. Especially beautiful scenery opens up from the ridge, where you can admire the Kaindy Canyon, the village of Saty, and the Chilik River valley. If you want to enjoy nature and serene waters, it is hard to imagine a better destination than a tour to Kaindy Lake.

    Kaindy Lake is located near the Kungey Alatau mountain range, close to the Kolsay Lakes. Tucked away in the middle of a dense forest, the road leading there is rugged. Surprisingly, despite its unique beauty, the lake remains less crowded, offering a peaceful atmosphere. Yet, year after year, more visitors come to admire this famous "sunken forest."

    Over time, rising waters flooded the gorge, leaving the dried coniferous treetops rising above the lake's turquoise surface. (See album “Kaindy”).

    Near Lake Kaindy, comfortable guest houses in the nearby village provide everything needed for a pleasant stay. Here, you can enjoy traditional Kazakh cuisine, including fresh lamb dishes or locally caught trout.

    Program: In one day, you will visit Charyn Canyon and Kolsay Lake, followed by an overnight stay in comfortable guest houses. The next morning, a scenic mountain drive takes you to the breathtaking Kaindy Lake. Upon arrival, you will have plenty of free time for a picnic, hiking, and exploring the area. After lunch, we depart for Almaty. You can discuss all meal preferences and picnic options in advance with our team. All vehicles are fully prepared for mountain terrain.
