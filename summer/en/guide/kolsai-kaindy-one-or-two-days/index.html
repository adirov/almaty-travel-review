Kolsai and Kaindy: one day or two?

Kazakhstan Travel · Prepared: 2 November 2026

Both options are available in the catalogue. The one-day express combines Kaindy, Kolsai and Charyn with an early start and a late return. The two-day itinerary splits the journey and includes an overnight stop in Saty.

  • What does the one-day trip involve?
  • What changes with two days?
  • Which option should you choose?
  • What should you confirm before booking?

What does the one-day trip involve?

The current itinerary lists hotel departure at 06:00 and return to Almaty at 23:00. Stops are Kaindy, Kolsai and Charyn Canyon, in that order. Expect a long day with driving and walking rather than a short city excursion. Treat the times as itinerary guidance and confirm arrangements for your travel date.

What changes with two days?

Day one covers Charyn Canyon, Kolsai-1 and an overnight stay in Saty. Day two covers Kaindy and the return to Almaty with stops at Black Canyon viewpoints. Confirm the accommodation and booking conditions when discussing the trip.

Which option should you choose?

Consider the express if you have only one free day and an early departure and late return suit you. Compare the overnight option if you have two days. Tell the manager about your group, preferred walking pace and any time constraints.

What should you confirm before booking?

Send your dates, party size, meeting point in Almaty and accommodation preferences. Ask about route availability, walking sections, optional horse riding, meals and the final list of services. Prices are provided by the manager rather than published on the website.
