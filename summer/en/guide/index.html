Almaty and Kazakhstan travel guide

Practical answers about tours from Almaty, Kolsai and Kaindy, Charyn Canyon and yurt stays.

Based on current itineraries and owner-supplied information. Confirm final arrangements with the manager.

Kolsai and Kaindy: one day or two?

Both options are available in the catalogue. The one-day express combines Kaindy, Kolsai and Charyn with an early start and a late return. The two-day itinerary splits the journey and includes an overnight stop in Saty.

Charyn Canyon: choosing an itinerary and walking level

Choose between a dedicated Charyn day trip and itineraries combining the canyon with the lakes. Walking time and effort depend on the programme; there is no single walking distance for every tour.

How to arrange a private tour from Almaty on WhatsApp

Send the manager your dates, party size, preferred destinations and available time. Prices are not published on the website: the price and services are confirmed for your request.

Yurt stays near Kolsai: what to confirm before travelling

The company offers yurt accommodation in the Kolsai area. The website shows photographs supplied by the owner. Confirm the exact location, facilities and availability for your dates before booking.
