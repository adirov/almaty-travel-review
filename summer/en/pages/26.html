Private Driver-Guide in Almaty

Book a private driver-guide in Almaty: you travel only with your own group in our 4x4 SUV, with an English-speaking driver-guide who meets you at your hotel, drives the route at your pace and tells you about the places on the way.

Use it for a private tour to Charyn Canyon, the Kolsai and Kaindy lakes or Altyn-Emel, for a day in the mountains around Almaty, or simply as transport with a driver-guide. The tour price includes the car and fuel, the driver-guide, park tickets and eco fees, water and hotel pickup; meals and accommodation are separate or come as an All inclusive package.

Send your dates, the number of travellers and the route on WhatsApp, and we will reply with a price.

Our cars on the road

We use our own 4x4 SUVs, suitable for mountain and gravel roads to the lakes and national parks.

Driver-guide or a full private tour?

A driver-guide drives our 4x4 and also shows you the places: he knows the roads, the trails and the best viewpoints, and speaks English. You can book him for one of our ready routes, for your own route, or simply as transport with a driver. A private tour is the same car and driver-guide plus a planned programme and, if you like, booked hotels and meals (All inclusive). Either way, only your group is in the car.

When a driver-guide is the best choice

  • You want to set your own pace and stops, for example for photography
  • You travel with children or older relatives and need a flexible day
  • You are combining several places, such as Charyn, Kolsai and Altyn-Emel, over a few days
  • You want local help with park tickets, roads and timing

Frequently asked questions

Does the driver-guide speak English?

Yes.

Is fuel included?

Yes. The price includes the car and all transport costs, the driver-guide, park tickets and eco fees, water and hotel pickup.

Do I need an SUV for Kolsai and Kaindy?

The roads to the lakes include mountain and gravel sections, which is why we use 4x4 vehicles.

Can the driver-guide stay with us for several days?

Yes, for multi-day routes such as the Golden Ring.

Can we change the route on the day?

Within reason, yes: discuss it with your driver-guide. Big changes are best agreed in advance on WhatsApp.

Are meals and hotels included?

No, they are paid separately or booked as the All inclusive package.
