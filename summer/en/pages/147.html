Helicopter and airplanes flights


 Do you love the mountains, and are you mesmerizingly drawn to their beauty and grandeur? Have you been dreaming of a helicopter tour to capture breathtaking, mind-opening views? Does the thrill of high altitudes and unknown horizons beckon you to soar and experience new sensations? Welcome aboard our helicopters!

Helicopter tours in Almaty are ready to make your dreams come true and let you witness the Dmitriev Glacier, the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains, Kolsay Lakes, Charyn Canyon, vast steppes, green foothills, and snow-capped peaks.

The altitude of the glacier is 4,230 meters above sea level. Its snow cover never melts, even in summer, offering skiing and snowboarding enthusiasts the unique opportunity to enjoy heli-skiing from May to August. The vertical drop of the skiing area is 1,200 meters, with a run length of up to 3,000 meters. We offer you 4 exciting descents and will transport you to the ski zone in less than 15 minutes!

 The flights are operated on Mi-17 and Mi-8MTV helicopters (2004–2007 models). Safety is achieved not only through high-quality aircraft equipment and certificates, but also through the professionalism of our pilots and guides — experienced skiers and snowboarders. Flights are operated in groups of 13–15 people. It is also possible to rent a Eurocopter designed for small private groups of 1 to 4 people.

 

We guarantee you a wonderful stay in Almaty, organize unforgettable helicopter tours, and make your day bright and full of amazing experiences.

Flight conditions: – Flight time to the heli-skiing area is 12–15 minutes. – Ski season runs from May to August inclusive. – Helicopters used: modified Mi-17 and Mi-8MTV (2004–2007 models). All Mi-8MTV helicopters have been fully tested and certified. – All pilots are professionals with 15 to 30 years of flight experience. – Guides are professional skiers and snowboarders. – Group size: 13–15 people. – Private rental of a small Eurocopter is available for groups of 1 to 4 people.
