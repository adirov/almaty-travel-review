The final price includes

  • Transport and all transport expenses (private SUV)
  • English-speaking driver-guide
  • Accommodation in the best hotels of the national parks (multi-day tours)
  • 3 meals a day in restaurants and hotels
  • Entrance tickets to national parks
  • Drinking water

Tailored to your request

  • Every tour can be customised: route, pace and stops are planned around you
  • Hiking is optional: we can add walks or leave them out completely
  • Suitable for any age, no restrictions
  • Transport with a driver-guide only is also available
  • Private trips and groups of up to 50 people
  • Kyrgyzstan can be added to any tour

The main season is summer. We also work in winter, depending on the weather and open roads; Charyn Canyon and the lakes are open all year.
