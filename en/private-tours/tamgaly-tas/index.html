Tamgaly Tas — Petroglyphs on the Ili River

Petroglyphs on Ili river

The rock art of the Ili River valley is one of the oldest monuments of Semirechye. Petroglyphs date back to the Bronze Age, the Early Iron Age and the Turkic period. A private 1-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal guide-driver. We never combine travelers into group tours.
