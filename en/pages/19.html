Paragliding

At 30 km from Almaty has a unique place called Ush-Konyr.
This place is open since 1980 for hang gliders and still attracts a lot of pilots and tourists from around the world.
The flight on a paraglide will be in tandem with an instructor.
Flight time from 15 to 90 minutes.
Age of a passenger 6-100 years.
Opportunity to study piloting the glider as a group and individually.

Price

  • 20 minutes of the flight, instructor, transfer  150 USD
  • Paragliding from the highest point of the ski Resort Shymbulak in tandem with instructor 150 USD (lift tickets are  not included)
  • Group more than two person discount
  • Transfer from city (check with manager)
