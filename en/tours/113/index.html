Butakovka waterfall (hiking tour)

Added: 15 january 2019

     When you head towards Medeu along Dostyk Avenue, there is a road turning left into Butakovka Gorge. Being so close to the city center, it surprises many visitors to find such pristine nature and a stunning waterfall right at the city's doorstep. This proximity to nature is truly unique to Almaty. The trekking path runs along a small mountain stream that leads directly to the Butakovka Waterfall. During the summer season, the trail is lined with wild rose berries, which you can pick and enjoy along the way.

    This trip can also be organized using mountain bikes.

    Here, a cozy forester’s house is at your service, and for a small additional fee, a traditional Russian sauna (banya) can be prepared for you. Upon request, you can rent a horse for a peaceful horseback riding session around Butakovka, or venture higher above the waterfalls, where the "Shybynsay Extreme" recreation base is located.

    Program of the tour: A short 30-minute drive from the city brings you to the picturesque Butakovka Gorge. From there, you can choose between a 2-hour guided horseback riding tour or a gentle hiking trail to the waterfall. Either way, it will be an unforgettable and accessible excursion surrounded by breathtaking landscapes. Picnics and dinners can be arranged in advance with our team.

    All tours can be organized on mountain bikes or horses.
