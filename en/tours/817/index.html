Kolsai Lakes, Kaindy and Charyn Canyon — 2-Day Private Tour from Almaty

2 days tour

A private 2-day jeep tour: just you, your group and a personal driver-guide. We never combine travelers into group tours.

Day 1

8:00 meeting in hotel/apartments

Departure to Charyn Canyon national park

11:30 arrival at Charyn Canyon national park

2.5 km hiking down the Canyon

13:00 lunch on the shore of the river in the Canyon,

going back on taxi

14:30 departure to Saty village and Kolsai lake 1

16:00 arrival at the Kolsai Lake 1

Free time/hiking/boating on lake/horseback riding

19:00 arrival at Saty village

Accommodation/dinner

Day 2

8:00 breakfast

9:00 departure to Kaindy lake

10:00 arrival at parking

3km hiking or horseback riding to the lake

12:00 departure to Saty village

13:00 lunch at Saty village

14:00 departure to Almaty city, stop on the way at the view points of Black Canyon

18:00 arrival at Almaty city

Charyn Canyon photos

Kaindy Lake

The final price includes

  • Private 4x4 SUV and all transport costs
  • English-speaking driver-guide
  • Pickup and drop-off at your hotel in Almaty
  • National park entrance tickets and eco fees
  • Drinking water

Meals and accommodation are paid separately, or choose the All inclusive package with hotels in the national parks and meals.

Tailored to your request

  • Every tour can be customised: route, pace and stops are planned around you
  • Hiking is optional: we can add walks or leave them out completely
  • Suitable for any age, no restrictions
  • Transport with a driver-guide only is also available
  • Private trips and groups of up to 50 people

The main season is summer. We also work in winter, depending on the weather and open roads; Charyn Canyon and the lakes are open all year.

Where you stay

The night is spent in Saty village, in a guesthouse or a yurt. Accommodation and meals are paid separately or booked as the All inclusive package; tell us which you prefer.

What to bring

Walking shoes for the canyon and the Kaindy trail, a warm layer for the evening at the lakes (Kolsai and Kaindy are higher and cooler than Almaty), a hat and sunscreen for Charyn, a small backpack and an overnight bag.

Frequently asked questions

Why two days instead of one?

The one-day express starts at 6:00 and ends around 23:00. With two days you have time to walk at each place and arrive in daylight.

How hard is the walking?

The canyon descent is 2.5 km downhill; you return by local transport. The Kaindy trail is about 3 km each way, or you can ride a horse.

Can we stay in a yurt?

Yes, yurt stays near Kolsai can be arranged for the night in Saty.

What is included?

The 4x4 and all transport costs, the English-speaking driver-guide, hotel pickup and drop-off, national park tickets and eco fees, and drinking water. Accommodation and meals are separate or All inclusive.

Can we add Altyn-Emel?

Yes: see the 3-day Golden Ring Express.
