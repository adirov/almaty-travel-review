Private SUV tours Almaty

Added: 17 june 2022
 Almaty is majestically called the "Southern Capital" of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Nestled at the foothills of the Trans-Ili Alatau, the city attracts tourists with the extraordinary beauty of its scenery, a mild climate, pristine nature, and rich historical heritage.

The diversity of flora and fauna, blossoming orchards, and unique nature reserves contribute greatly to the development of tourism in Almaty. No wonder the city is called the pearl of Zhetysu (Semirechye). Visiting Almaty, you will not only immerse yourself in the vibrant cultural life of the city but also discover important historical monuments and scientific centers of the Southern Capital.

Kazakhstan Travel Agency offers a wide variety of holidays and organizes unforgettable tours in Almaty.

Lush green parks, historical monuments, cultural centers, art galleries, and architectural ensembles — this is only a partial list of the attractions that draw thousands of tourists and visitors.

The motto of travel agencies in Almaty is quality and excellent service. Every team member is an experienced professional in the field of tourism. You will be pleasantly surprised not only by the high quality of service and individual approach, but also by the seamless organization of your trip. The perfect combination of price and service will ensure a pleasant experience and make you want to visit this city again and again.

A wealth of natural resources, cultural and historical heritage, extraordinary landscapes, and captivating scenery have had a positive impact on the scale and pace of tourism development in Almaty for many years. The majestic Tien Shan mountains preserve the history and traditions of local tourism, while the emerald waters of rivers and lakes reflect the unforgettable emotions and vivid impressions of the city's guests.

Kazakhstan Travel Agency will help you create unforgettable tours in Almaty and beyond, introducing you to the vibrant nightlife and offering relaxing getaways in the heart of nature.

Ski resorts near the city will satisfy all lovers of winter sports and outdoor activities. You can conquer ski slopes, feel the adrenaline, and enjoy the purity of snow-covered expanses among fresh coniferous forests. A hot air balloon ride will reveal breathtaking views of mountain landscapes and snow-covered valleys.

With Kazakhstan Travel Agency, you can book sightseeing tours in Almaty, experience unforgettable emotions from horseback riding, or take an exciting quad bike ride. The unique flora and fauna of these places also attract fishing and hunting enthusiasts. The fabulous beauty of rivers and lakes, the majesty of mountains and plains, and picturesque landscapes will leave a lasting impression on every traveler.

Almaty proudly holds the title of the Southern Capital, and every year the number of visitors wanting to take excursions in Almaty and touch the history and culture of the region continues to grow.

Historical relics, architectural monuments, natural richness, and a high level of hospitality make Almaty a top attraction for tourists from all over the world.

Planning and booking

Are these private tours?

The listed jeep programs are private: you travel with your own group and a guide-driver, rather than being combined with other travellers.

Is guiding available in English?

English-speaking guiding is available. Confirm the arrangements for your dates when requesting a quote.

How is the price confirmed?

Send your dates, group size and preferred route. Ask for a written quote specifying the vehicle, accommodation, meals, park fees and any optional activities before booking.

What should I check before paying?

Confirm availability, the final itinerary, the complete price and the applicable payment, cancellation and weather arrangements with the operator.
