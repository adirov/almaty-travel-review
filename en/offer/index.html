Public offer agreement (booking and service terms)

This document is an official proposal (public offer) from IE «KAZAKHSTAN TRAVEL AGENCY» (hereinafter — the «Provider») to any individual or legal entity (hereinafter — the «Customer») that accepts this offer under the conditions specified below.

In accordance with Article 395 of the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, unconditional acceptance of the terms of this public offer is the Customer making a partial or full prepayment for tourism services via an individual link (invoice) generated by the Provider and sent to the Customer by e-mail or messenger.

1. Subject of the agreement

1.1. The Provider undertakes, at the request of the Customer, to organize and provide tourism services (including, but not limited to: individual jeep tours, sightseeing programs, off-road vehicle transport, a private driver-guide) along an agreed route in the Republic of Kazakhstan, and the Customer undertakes to pay for these services under the terms of this Agreement.

1.2. Descriptions of basic tour programs and possible locations are presented on the website almaty-travel.kz; each final route is formed individually based on the Customer’s requests.

2. Payment, booking and settlement procedure

2.1. All of the Provider’s jeep tours are individual. The final route, schedule, list of included services and total cost of the tour are calculated by the Provider personally for each Customer after preliminary coordination.

2.2. After the individual program and tour cost are approved by the Customer, the Provider generates a personal payment link (electronic invoice) via the TipTop Pay payment system and sends it to the Customer.

2.3. To finalize the booking of a tour and secure the dates, the Customer makes a prepayment of 30% of the total estimated tour cost via the provided link within 3 banking days from the moment of receipt.

2.4. The remaining 70% of the tour cost is paid by the Customer in cash on the first day of the tour before departure.

2.5. All non-cash payments via individual links are accepted in the national currency of the Republic of Kazakhstan — tenge (KZT). International clients pay the invoice with Visa/Mastercard cards in their own currency, with automatic conversion to tenge at the exchange rate of the Customer’s issuing bank at the time of the transaction.

2.6. Unless the individual program says otherwise, the tour price includes a private 4x4 vehicle and transport costs, an English-speaking driver-guide, pickup and drop-off at the Customer’s hotel in Almaty, national park entrance tickets and eco fees, and drinking water. Meals and accommodation are paid separately or included as an agreed All inclusive package.

3. Rights and obligations of the parties

3.1. The Provider shall: provide the services in strict accordance with the agreed individual tour program; provide prepared and functional off-road vehicles and qualified driver-guides; inform the Customer about safety rules in difficult terrain, mountain and steppe sections of the route.

3.2. The Customer shall: provide accurate passport and contact details for all tour participants (including data for border-zone permits and travel insurance, if the route requires them); observe safety rules inside the vehicle and at stops and treat the Provider’s equipment with care; respect the laws, culture and customs of the places visited.

4. Cancellation and refund policy

4.1. If the Customer cancels, the prepayment made via the link is refunded minus the Provider’s actual expenses that cannot be cancelled (including, but not limited to: non-refundable hotel/guesthouse bookings, purchased supplies, fees for permits to enter national parks or border zones).

4.2. If the Customer cancels the tour less than 7 days before its start, the non-cash prepayment is retained by the Provider in full as a penalty for the loss of the vehicle and crew booking.

4.3. Prepayments are refunded only to the bank card used for the payment via TipTop Pay, within 5–10 banking days. No refund applies to the cash portion of the payment if it has not yet been paid.

5. Force majeure and liability

5.1. The parties are exempt from liability for partial or complete non-fulfilment of obligations caused by force majeure: natural disasters (mudslides, rockfalls, washed-out roads), sudden weather changes in mountain areas that make jeep travel dangerous, or closure of roads, border zones or national parks by government order.

5.2. The Provider is not responsible for changes in the tour schedule caused by delays at checkpoints, national park entrances or road closures beyond the Provider’s control.
