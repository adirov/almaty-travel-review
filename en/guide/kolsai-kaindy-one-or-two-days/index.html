Kolsai and Kaindy from Almaty: One Day or Two?

Kazakhstan Travel · Prepared: 2 November 2026

The one-day express combines Kaindy, Kolsai and Charyn with an early start and a late return. The two-day tour splits the journey with a night in Saty village.

  • What does the one-day trip involve?
  • What changes with two days?
  • Which option should you choose?
  • What should you confirm before booking?

What does the one-day trip involve?

The current itinerary lists hotel departure at 06:00 and return to Almaty at 23:00. Stops are Kaindy, Kolsai and Charyn Canyon, in that order. Expect a long day with driving and walking rather than a short city excursion.

What changes with two days?

Day one covers Charyn Canyon, Kolsai-1 and an overnight stay in Saty. Day two covers Kaindy and the return to Almaty with stops at Black Canyon viewpoints. You stay in a guesthouse or a yurt; accommodation and meals are paid separately or booked as the All inclusive package.

Which option should you choose?

Consider the express if you have only one free day and an early departure and late return suit you. Compare the overnight option if you have two days. Tell the manager about your group, preferred walking pace and any time constraints.

What should you confirm before booking?

Send your dates, party size, meeting point in Almaty and accommodation preferences. Ask about route availability, walking sections, optional horse riding, meals and the final list of services. We reply with a price for your group.
