Charyn Canyon: choosing an itinerary and walking level

Kazakhstan Travel · Prepared: 2 November 2026

Choose between a dedicated Charyn day trip and itineraries combining the canyon with the lakes. Walking time and effort depend on the programme; there is no single walking distance for every tour.

  • Charyn alone or a combined trip?
  • How much walking is involved?
  • How should you prepare?
  • What needs confirmation?

Charyn alone or a combined trip?

The dedicated day trip focuses on the canyon. The express with Kaindy and Kolsai combines several distant stops in one day. Compare the sequence and driving times as well as the list of sights.

How much walking is involved?

The two-day Charyn, Kolsai and Kaindy itinerary lists a 2.5 km descent into the canyon and a return uphill by local taxi. This applies to that itinerary, not automatically to every trip. Confirm your own walking section and return transport.

How should you prepare?

Ask about the forecast and route conditions for your date. Useful points to discuss include suitable footwear, sun protection, drinking water and clothing for the weather. Mention any difficulty with descents or uphill walking before agreeing on a programme.

What needs confirmation?

Send dates, group size, meeting point and preferred activity level. Confirm meals, transport inside the canyon, return time and the final price. A dedicated day trip and a multi-day itinerary may have different arrangements.
