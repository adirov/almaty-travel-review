A private tour means the car, the route and the day belong only to your group. Below is what the tour price covers, what you pay separately and how to get a quote.

## What the tour price includes

- A private 4x4 SUV and all transport costs for the route
- An English-speaking driver-guide for the whole trip
- Pickup from and drop-off at your hotel in Almaty
- National park entrance tickets and eco fees
- Drinking water in the car

## What is paid separately

Meals and accommodation are not part of the basic tour price. You can pay for them on the way, or ask for the **All inclusive** package: then we book the hotels or guesthouses in the national parks and the meals for you, and they are added to the quote.

## Can I change the route?

Yes. Every itinerary can be changed: the route, the pace and the stops are planned around you. Hiking is optional, so we can add walks or leave them out. There is no age limit. If you only need transport, you can book [a private driver-guide and car](/en/pages/26.html) without a full tour.

## How much does a private tour cost?

Prices are not published on the site because every tour is calculated for your dates, group size and route. Send us your dates, the number of travellers and the places you want to see on WhatsApp, and we will reply with a price and a plan.
